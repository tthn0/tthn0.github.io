Software Engineer & Web Developer

Hello, my name Thomas! I'm full-stack software engineer and web developer with experience shipping real-world code and a strong passion for designing beautiful user interfaces.

Through my professional work, internships, coursework, and personal projects, I have gained expertise various programming languages, including TypeScript/JavaScript, Python, PHP, Java, and many others. I'm eager to contribute my expertise and knowledge to the field of computer science, and I am always seeking new opportunities to learn and grow!
